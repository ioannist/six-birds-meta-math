# Paper Proposal — Six Birds Foundations IV

## Working title

**Six Birds Foundations IV: A Catalog of Layer-Agnostic Structural Laws**

Alternatives:
- *Six Birds Foundations IV: Normal-Form Theorems for Formed Layers*

## One-paragraph pitch

The Six Birds Foundations stack (I = primitive closure mechanics; II = seven-schema admissibility + quotient packaging; III = finite audited interaction calculus with BirdInt judgments) provides the primitive machinery on which all framework-internal results rest. Foundations IV elevates substructures already fragmentary in the corpus into named **structural laws** stated in a uniform normal-form style: each law has a setup, theorem statement, proof spine, normal-form case enumeration, relationship-to-corpus map, BirdInt status, and explicit nonclaims. The catalog is organized along the eight primitive-necessity axes recorded in the statement source map: **access, closure (a.k.a. stability), transport, status, records, symmetry, interfaces, self-reference**. The monograph groups these eight atlas axes into six drafting chapters as follows: Access; Stability (= the "closure" atlas axis); Transport; Symmetry; Status / Records / Coherence (atlas axes status + records + interfaces, grouped for drafting coherence); Self-reference and Limits (atlas axis self-reference plus limit-shaped laws). The catalog currently contains ~36 mature theorem-grade laws plus a small set of sketches awaiting recognition-source content (e.g. spontaneous symmetry-breaking). Several anchor laws are already in print as substrate-specific instances: F6 (No-Needles Stability) is the spine of Paper 4 (Needle Killer) and Paper 5 (NS); F13 (Hiddenness) carries two layers — a theorem-grade quotient/currentizer normal form, instantiated by Paper 7 (PvNP) on the saturated SAT layer, and a CSL formed-closure law at diagnosis grade, instantiated by Paper 8 (CSL); F14 (Duality Fixity) is the Symmetry-axis spine of Papers 2 (SDTC) and 6 (RH); F53 (Closure–Reality Boundary) is functionally Paper 3 (AOR). Foundations IV gives the layer-agnostic statements those papers instantiate, plus ~32 additional normal-form laws not yet in any landed paper. This paper proposes either a monograph (50–100 pages) or a thematic split into ~4–6 thematic papers along axis lines.

## Scope

**In**: ~36 mature normal-form theorem-grade laws, with proof spines and case enumerations tracked by the paper inventory and statements-of-record registry, organized by axis. Each law is stated layer-agnostically; substrate-specific instances are referenced as illustrations, not re-proved.

**Out**:
- Sketches awaiting recognition-source content (spontaneous symmetry-breaking, vacuum non-emptiness as standalone, etc.) — listed as future work.
- Substrate-specific proofs that already live in landed papers (paper 4, 5, 7, 8, 2, 6, 3) — referenced as instances, not duplicated.
- Recognition sources themselves (closure-content discipline is documented; specific sources like `Γ_csl-sat-hidden` or `Γ_SDTC^Selberg` are not derived here — they come from the substrate-track papers).
- Adequacy-residual Schur calculus per se (Paper 1) — used as black-box machinery.

## Packaging decision (open)

Two packaging options to decide before drafting:

**Option A — One monograph (recommended starting point).** Single 50–100 page document presenting all ~36 laws under one cover, organized by axis. Pros: coherent meta-narrative; cross-axis dependencies handled in one place; easier to cite as a single Foundations IV reference. Cons: long; some axes (Status/Records/Coherence has ~25 laws) overweight relative to others.

**Option B — Thematic split (~4–6 papers).** Natural splits along axis groupings:
- *Foundations IV-A: Access and Stability* (Quotientality, Adequacy/No-Overread, No-Free-Distinction, Layer Multiplicity, Boundary of Knowability, No-Needles, Sufficiency Closure, Strict Extension, No Unstatused Residual) — ~9 laws.
- *Foundations IV-B: Transport* (Descent-Repair, Holonomy-Memory Repair, Local-Global Obstruction, Recombination Comparison / Branch-Merge) — 4 laws with drop-in LaTeX appendices already drafted.
- *Foundations IV-C: Symmetry* (Symmetry-Breaking / Branch Selection, Anomaly, Duality Equivalence) — 3 laws (plus Duality Fixity / Trace Confinement which lives in Paper 2/6).
- *Foundations IV-D: Status, Records, and Coherence* (Objectivity as Common Quotient, Lawhood Descent, Object Persistence, Fact/Record Formation, Reflexive Nonclosure, Interface Mediation, Probability/Causality/Moduli, Conservation as Orbit Descent, Composability, Presentation Invariance, Explanation/Counterfactual Legitimacy, etc.) — ~20+ laws (likely needs further split into IV-D1, IV-D2).
- *Foundations IV-E: Self-reference and Limits* (Horizon Sufficiency, Information Loss, Scale Descent/Renormalization, Singular Limit Completion, Complementarity, Arrow from Record Monotonicity, Entropy as Fiber Volume, Irreducibility, Continuum Emergence, Criticality, Locality, Law-State Split, Fine-Tuning, Topology, Common-Source, Vacuum, Unification, Closure Completeness Boundary).

Decision deferred to first complete draft; default = monograph, with axis-split spin-offs as the natural fallback if monograph exceeds 100 pages.

## Explicit nonclaims

- Not a substrate-specific landing of any Clay-class or other open problem; the substrate-track papers (5 NS, 6 RH, 7 PvNP) carry those.
- Not a derivation of recognition sources; those are imported by the substrate-track papers per the closure-content thesis (documented in the meta-math paper).
- Not a foundational reset of Foundations I/II/III; F-IV builds on them as black-box.
- Not a unification claim; the eight axes are an organizational scheme, not a categorical structure.
- Not a complete catalog: the sketches (SSB, dimension, smoothness) and any future axis additions remain open.
