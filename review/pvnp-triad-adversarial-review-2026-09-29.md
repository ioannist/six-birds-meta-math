## Strength and failure tests

1. **Shared mathematics:** the observation operator, nonlinear frame, XI projection shape, clause residual transport, and SAT branch commutator are explicit equations. This exceeds a shared vocabulary or three renamed propositions.
2. **No cross-domain closure implication:** the conjunction has no map taking an RH zero-window XI charge into a SAT witness-currentization certificate, or vice versa. It has no map paying Navier terminal/reconstruction costs from a SAT residual. RH reflection annihilates the common native probe, while satisfiable SAT gives a positive native mean. `ProbePreservingTransportNoGo.no_probe_preserving_transport_to_sat` now formally rules out any map that both preserves this probe on every state and sends a reflected RH displacement to a satisfiable SAT indicator. This is a narrow obstruction to that proposed transport, not a no-go for other probes, states, or closure relations.
3. **Generic-filter ablation:** `filteredPositiveMass_pos_iff` holds for an arbitrary Boolean filter. Thus Gaussian positivity alone does not use SAT's special structure. SAT-specific content enters through the verified CNF weight, the clause failure subtraction, the unit-mask operation, the XI commutator, and the encoded-language return. The existing equations do not show these SAT operations correspond to a native RH zero-window operation.
4. **Resource boundary:** the assignment carrier has `2^n` points. Exact positivity of its Gaussian sum is an information statement, and no polynomial precision/evaluation theorem is present. Hiddenness now proves a fixed all-input polynomial TM for raw SAT search *given* a polynomial SAT decider; its query envelope, actual wrapper costs, and `FP` admission are checked. This reduction does not evaluate the Gaussian sum or make its exact positivity test efficient. It also does not generate a decider from the Gaussian observation.

This prevents treating an empty or target-tuned `E_Q_bang` as a free source of separation. The triad operator does not independently derive the recognition content or application-specific admission law.

## Verdict and next theorem gate

The construction establishes a mathematically checked **shared observation and residual span** with domain returns. It does **not** establish one sourced strong closure condition for the three problems, a cross-domain implication between their closure costs, or a polynomial Gaussian SAT solver. The present theorem is assembled from genuine domain equations, but its outer conjunction alone is not an intertwining theorem between RH and SAT closures. It should be presented at the operator/return level unless a stronger relation is proved.
